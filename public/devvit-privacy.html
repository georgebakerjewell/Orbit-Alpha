Orbit Alpha News (Reddit app): Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy covers the Orbit Alpha News app on the Reddit Developer Platform.

What the app collects

None. The app does not collect, store or share personal data about Reddit users. It does not read users' posts, comments or messages.

What the app stores

The app keeps a short-lived list of IDs for news stories it has already posted (deleted automatically after 30 days), so it does not post the same story twice. This list contains no personal data.

External requests

The app makes one type of external request: it fetches a public list of news headlines from www.orbitalpha.cloud. No Reddit user information is sent in that request.

Reddit

Reddit's own Privacy Policy applies to your use of Reddit.
